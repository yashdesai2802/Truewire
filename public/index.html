TrueWire Verification Architecture

TrueWire continuously aggregates live feeds from Indian broadcasters (Aaj Tak, ABP News, NDTV India, Zee News, News18 Hindi, India TV, Dainik Jagran, Amar Ujala, BBC Hindi, DD News).

Multi-Stage Workflow: 1) Extract core factual claim. 2) Entity recognition. 3) Corroboration against 80+ live wire articles & search. 4) Synthesis of supported, misleading, or unverified verdicts.

English UI Guarantee: While Hindi headlines are maintained in Devanagari for journalistic authenticity, all verification analysis, verdicts, and evidence reports are synthesized in English.
